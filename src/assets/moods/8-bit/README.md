# 8-bit Mood background

`garden.png` was generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-09 for Crux Garden's 8-bit Mood. It ships locally and is ingested into the Blob Store when applied, so saved/exported Mood Packages carry the image.

Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide desktop wallpaper for Crux Garden's 8-bit Mood. Create a beautiful authentic 8-bit pixel-art garden at twilight, like an inviting quiet location in a classic cartridge adventure. A little winding stone path, dark leafy trees and ferns framing the edges, small mint-green plants and tiny warm golden lights, a distant low garden wall, dark blue starry sky. Restrained midnight navy (#101428), slate blue, mint (#86efac), warm pale gold (#ffd36b). Hand-placed square pixels, consistent coarse pixel grid, hard edges, flat limited colors and selective dithering; absolutely no photographic rendering, blur, smooth gradients or modern 3D. Wide landscape 16:9 composition. Keep the middle half spacious and subdued, with low contrast lawn and sky so application panels and text can sit over it. Most decorative detail at the left/right edges and lower corners. Calm and playful, a garden one wants to spend time in. No characters, no UI, no lettering, no logos, no watermark.

The theme uses the locally hosted [Press Start 2P font](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/pressstart2p) for display text. Its original font and SIL OFL license are in `public/fonts/PressStart2P-Regular.ttf` and `public/fonts/PressStart2P-OFL.txt`. Body text uses JetBrains Mono.
