A blank canvas can ask too much of you. A small, useful starting point asks a kinder question: what would you change first?

That is why a new Garden can begin with a home page. Put your name on it. Add a photo. Write one honest sentence about yourself. You have already made something that did not exist a moment ago.

## Keep the story

When it feels right, mark a version in Growth. The point is not to freeze your work. It is to make the next experiment easier to begin.

Try another title. Move something. Come back to an earlier version to see how your thinking changed.

## Share is a choice

Some things are for the world. Others are just for you. Preview locally, keep a private copy, and publish only when it makes sense for the creation.

A useful tool should leave you with more room to make things—not more work to explain yourself.
