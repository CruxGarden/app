## Get the desktop app

Crux Garden is being prepared for its first public desktop release. There is not yet a public installer on the [official releases page](https://github.com/CruxGarden/app/releases). Use that page for release availability and platform instructions; do not download unofficial installers. If you already have a testing build, use the launch instructions that came with it.

The desktop targets macOS, Windows and Linux. Build and runtime checks are not a guarantee for every device. A release's notes will identify its supported versions and limitations. Developers can use the [repository's contributor instructions](https://github.com/CruxGarden/app#development).

## Your first launch

1. Open the app and choose **Enter** (the circle-plus mark).
2. Choose **Plant a new garden**. Your Garden is a place for your projects on your computer. Restore is for people bringing back existing work.
3. Choose where your Garden lives. You may leave username, photo and account connection for later. **AI Tools can stay disabled.**
4. Leave **Include a first home page walkthrough** checked, then choose **Welcome**.
5. Open **Hello, world** and follow **Make it yours → See your page → Watch it grow → Share it**. Start with [your first home page](../first-home/).

## What do I need?

| What you want to do | What you need |
| --- | --- |
| Read this bundled guide, edit local files, keep history | The app; no account or AI key |
| Preview an Astro site for the first time | Internet access to install that project's dependencies |
| Ask a remote AI collaborator to work | Enable AI Tools in Settings and configure a supported provider; provider charges may apply |
| Publish or sync to crux.garden | Internet access and a crux.garden account; the service's plan limits apply |

## Find your way back

**Help** in the top bar opens the guide without leaving your project. You can also search **help** with **⌘K** on macOS or **Ctrl+K** on Windows/Linux. Close the guide to return to your work.

**Panels** opens the panel picker: Workshop shows your creation, Artifacts holds files, Growth is history, and Tasks keeps independent work. The Garden name in the top bar takes you back toward your projects. You do not need every panel at once.

Start with one small project. [Tasks and agents](../../guides/tasks/) can wait until you want to try more than one thing at a time.
