## Mark a meaningful moment

Growth is your Crux’s version history. Open **Growth → Mark version** and add a short label before a major change: “Before the redesign” is more useful than “Version 12”.

AI turns also create automatic snapshots. A marked version gives your own work a memorable point in the history.

## Inspect before restoring

Select an earlier version to inspect it. A historical view is read-only; looking at it does not mean you have restored it. Return to your current work when you finish comparing.

Before restoring, mark your current state. Review the restore action carefully so you know which files will become current. For risky experiments, a separate Task can keep work apart until you review it.

## History is not an off-device backup

Local Growth helps you revisit changes. It does not protect against losing the computer or the disk. Export a private archive of important Cruxes or a Garden backup and keep another copy somewhere you trust.

Publishing shares a selected public result. It does not replace that private backup.
