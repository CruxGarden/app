You don’t need a finished idea. Or an AI key. Start with something small and let it grow.

Crux Garden is a local-first creative workspace. A **Crux** holds your creation, its files, and its history. A **Garden** brings your Cruxes together.

## Your first five minutes

1. **Make a home page.** Start with Hello, world, add your name and a photo, and see a real site take shape.
2. **Keep a version.** Open Growth and mark a moment you want to come back to.
3. **Share when you’re ready.** Preview privately first. Publishing is an explicit step.

## Yours, from the beginning

Your Project Folders live on your computer. You can edit files directly, work through a Crux Tool, or invite an AI collaborator. AI is optional; remote AI, first-time site dependency installation, and publishing need a connection.

A published website is **not a backup** of your Garden. Keep a private export of work you care about.
