# Play and customize One Big Sky

Open **Clean** in Workshop to play. Click Start, press W to join, add a bot with +, press W again to ready, then use Enter to choose a mode and launch. A/D move, W flaps, S dives and E boosts. Escape pauses; moving focus away pauses a match. The upstream README lists the other keyboard seats and controller controls.

Matches and first-to-three series are held in memory. Reloading or closing the game starts fresh; there are no saved player profiles or persistent high scores. Growth records changes to the game sources, not live match state.

Use Advanced to edit the sources, or start a Task and ask an agent to change the game. Restore a Growth checkpoint to recover an earlier version of your rules, art or interface. Export the complete Crux to back up its source and history. Playing locally does not require Share website; an optional static website serves the game with a separate match in each visitor's browser, not online multiplayer.

Apply **One Big Sky** from the Mood library for its sky background, arcade colors and Skye persona. Moods affect the whole Garden; applying one does not change the game or silently follow individual Cruxes.
