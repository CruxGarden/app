# Welcome to the garden

This is a digital garden: a collection of notes that grow over time rather than a feed of finished posts. Nothing here is done; everything here is tended.

## Where to start

- [[What is a digital garden]] — the idea in a page.
- [[growth-stages|Growth stages]] — how a seedling becomes evergreen.

## How it works

Every note is a Markdown file. Link notes to each other with `[[double brackets]]`; the note you link to shows this one under *Linked from*, and the [graph](/graph) draws the whole garden. Press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> to search.
