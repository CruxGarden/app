Every note in this garden carries a `growthStage` in its frontmatter. The stage is a promise to the reader about how much to trust the page.

- **seedling** — a first draft, a hunch, a quote worth keeping. Expect gaps.
- **budding** — revisited at least once; the shape is there, the edges are rough.
- **evergreen** — tended many times; safe to link from elsewhere.

Move a note up when you come back and it still holds. Move it down when you learn it was wrong; that is gardening too. The [graph](/graph) colours notes by stage, so an unfinished corner of the garden is easy to spot.
