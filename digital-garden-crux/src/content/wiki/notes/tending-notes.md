- **Link as you write.** When a sentence mentions an idea that deserves its own page, wrap it in `[[brackets]]` even if the page does not exist yet. The graph shows the gaps.
- **Revisit before you add.** A returning visit to an old note is worth more than a new seedling.
- **Prune.** Delete what you no longer believe. A garden with fewer, truer notes reads better than one with everything.
- **Keep the gate open.** Tending in public keeps the writing honest; see [[Learning in public]].
