Welcome. This is a blog grown in Crux Garden on the Astro Cactus theme: fast, plain, and yours.

Posts live in `content/posts/` as Markdown with a little frontmatter (a title, a description, a date, tags). Add one from the Builder or ask your collaborator to draft it. Notes are the short form, at `content/notes/`; tags collect posts under `/tags/`.

Press the search icon to search the whole site (it is indexed when the blog is shared). Switch the theme with the sun and moon at the top. Everything else, from the header links to the about page, is a file you can change.
