# Terms of Service

**This is a scaffold, not legal advice.** It lists the headings a terms page
usually needs so you can see the shape. Replace every section with your own
words, or with wording your solicitor gives you, before you share the site.

## Who we are

Your business name, where it is registered, and how to contact you.

## What we provide

The service or work these terms cover, and what they do not cover.

## What we ask of you

How the site and the service may be used, and what is not allowed.

## Payment

Prices, when payment is due, and what happens if it is late.

## Cancellation and refunds

How either side ends the arrangement, and what is refundable.

## Liability

What you are and are not responsible for. This is the section most worth
taking advice on.

## Changes to these terms

How you will tell people when these terms change.

## Governing law

Which country's law applies and where disputes are settled.
