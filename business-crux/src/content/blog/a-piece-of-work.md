The most useful post a business can write is about work it has actually done.

**The problem.** What the client came with, in their words if you can.

**What we did.** The decisions worth explaining, not every step.

**What changed.** A number if you have one, a quote if you do not.

One real example of this is worth a page of claims about yourself.
