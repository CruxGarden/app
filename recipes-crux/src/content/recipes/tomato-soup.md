## Ingredients

- 1.2 kg ripe tomatoes, halved
- 1 onion, quartered
- 4 cloves garlic, unpeeled
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 500 ml vegetable stock
- A handful of basil
- Salt, pepper, a pinch of sugar

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Put the tomatoes, onion and garlic on a tray, pour over the oil, season, and roast for 35 minutes until the edges char.
2. Squeeze the garlic out of its skins. Tip everything, juices included, into a pan with the stock.
3. Simmer for 10 minutes, then blend until smooth. Taste: a pinch of sugar if the tomatoes were sharp.
4. Tear in the basil and serve with bread.
