## Ingredients

- 100 g sunflower seeds
- 60 g pumpkin seeds
- 40 g sesame seeds
- 30 g chia seeds
- 250 ml water
- 1 tsp flaky salt

## Method

1. Stir all the seeds, the salt and the water together and leave for 15 minutes until the chia thickens it.
2. Spread thinly on a lined tray, right to the edges. Score into squares.
3. Bake at 160 °C for 30 minutes, flip the sheet, and bake 25 to 30 minutes more until crisp.
4. Cool, snap along the lines, keep in a tin for a fortnight.
